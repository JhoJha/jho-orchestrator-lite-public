# Product Context

Jho Lite was developed while the primary engineering priority was `client-product`.

The product pressure is important context: it explains why the orchestration project was deliberately frozen at a small, useful stage rather than expanded indefinitely.

The intended relationship is:

```text
Jho Lite
   ↓
helps inspect/review/plan
   ↓
client-product
   ↓
real engineering outcomes
   ↓
telemetry/evidence
   ↓
future orchestration decisions
```

The orchestrator is a tool for shipping the product, not a reason to stop shipping the product.
