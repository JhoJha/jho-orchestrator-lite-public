## AGY

Use the **official Google Antigravity CLI**.

Do not use a third-party OAuth bridge for Antigravity credentials.

AGY adapter requirements:

- headless;
- stream-json;
- explicit wire model;
- effort mapping;
- sandbox;
- workspace confinement;
- permission-denial parsing;
- no silent success after blocked required tools.
